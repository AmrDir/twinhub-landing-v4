## Design Context

### Users
Corporate clients, startups, and organizations in Egypt and the MENA region seeking professional media production, video editing, and AI-driven content generation solutions. They expect high-quality, reliable, and cutting-edge digital presence.

### Brand Personality
Professional, innovative, cinematic, and reliable. The brand aims to balance the creative flair of media production with the cutting-edge, technical precision of AI solutions.

### Aesthetic Direction
A modern, cinematic, bilingual (Arabic/English) interface. It should feel premium and slightly "tech-forward" but accessible. It likely needs a dark-mode leaning or high-contrast theme to make media assets pop, avoiding overly generic corporate templates.

### Design Principles
1. **Cinematic First**: Media, gallery, and video backgrounds should be the hero, with the UI supporting them gracefully.
2. **Clear Rhythm & Space**: Sections need distinct visual rhythm and breathing room, avoiding cluttered or overly dense layouts.
3. **Intentional Typography**: Strong hierarchy for section headings and clear, legible body text for both English and Arabic (RTL support).
4. **Subtle & Purposeful Motion**: Use motion only for entrances, exits, and state changes to enhance the premium feel, not to distract.
